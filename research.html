The Role of Vocational Education in Enhancing Women's Employment in Barishal City

Independent Research  ·  Barishal, Bangladesh  Under Review

Investigated the relationship between vocational training and women's employment outcomes in Barishal City. Designed and administered a structured questionnaire to 400 women using Kobo Toolbox. Analyzed the relationship between training satisfaction and outcomes using Logistic and Linear Regression Models in SPSS 17.

My Contributions

  • Logistic and Linear Regression analysis in SPSS 17
  • Training satisfaction vs. outcome modeling

My Contributions

  • Logistic and Poisson Regression analysis in STATA 14
  • Thematic analysis of coping strategies in NVivo 15
