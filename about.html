Biography

Md. Saifullah
Al Taheri

I'm an economics graduate from the University of Barishal with a CGPA of 3.58/4.0 and 4+ years of experience spanning academic research, consulting, business development, and digital creative work.

Born and raised in Sylhet, I moved to Barishal for my undergraduate studies and developed a deep interest in development economics — particularly how policy, environment, and social factors shape the lives of communities in coastal Bangladesh.

Beyond research, I've built a parallel track in digital literacy: designing graphics with Adobe Suite, building websites with HTML/CSS and WordPress, managing social media, and applying SEO — skills that let me communicate complex ideas in compelling, accessible ways.

I believe the best work happens at the intersection of rigorous analysis and creative expression. Whether writing a research paper or designing a poster, I bring the same attention to detail and care for impact.
